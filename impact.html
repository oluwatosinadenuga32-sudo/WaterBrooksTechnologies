Key impact & milestones

We've worked directly with farming communities to introduce sustainable storage practices and our solar-powered systems eliminate the need for fossil fuels, cutting carbon emissions along the way.

Impacted about 200 farmers across different farming communities in Oyo State.

Conducted a feasibility study at Akinyele Market, the largest market in Southwest Nigeria.

Secured 18 plots of land valued at approximately $20,000.

Engaged key stakeholders from government, private sector, and NGOs to build long-term partnerships.
